## Projects

### [US Equity Screener](https://github.com/joeltaipe12-del/us-equity-screener)

A five-factor screener and backtest over the S&P 100 (2020–2025). Pulled prices and fundamentals with Python, built a PostgreSQL star schema, scored every company on value, quality, momentum, risk and growth using SQL window functions, and backtested the top 10 against the S&P 500 — with the biases behind the headline result documented.

**Stack:** Python · PostgreSQL · Tableau · [Live dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/joel.taipe/viz/USEquityScreener/Screener)

### [AI Adoption in the Fortune 500](https://github.com/joeltaipe12-del/ai-adoption-fortune500)

A consulting-style analysis of AI maturity (2020–2025) using a synthetic dataset of 1,000 firms, 20 of them real companies. Separated real from synthetic records, remodelled the flat file into a star schema, answered six business questions in SQL and built three Tableau dashboards — flagging seven high-revenue, low-maturity firms as consulting targets.

**Stack:** PostgreSQL · Tableau

### [Ecuador Economic Analysis](https://github.com/joeltaipe12-del/ecuador-economic-analysis)

Research into four decades of Ecuador's economy — how growth, poverty and inequality moved across policy eras, and whether dollarisation helped or deepened rural and indigenous disparities.

**Stack:** Excel · World Bank / IMF / UNDP data
